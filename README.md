# 🧠 AI & ML Timelines

A visual timeline of major milestones in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning — created by **Shivani Mandora** as part of academic and professional reflection.

![AI & ML Timelines](Visual%20Presentation%20of%20AI-ML%20Timeline.jpg)

## 📘 Overview
This timeline traces the evolution of AI — from Alan Turing’s 1950 question *“Can machines think?”* to the 2020s expansion of AI with cloud and deep learning technologies.

Each milestone highlights:
- The **key event or discovery**
- Its **significance in shaping AI**
- The **key contributors or organizations**

## 🎯 Purpose
To present the progression of AI in a **visually engaging, simplified way** for learners, developers, and enthusiasts.  
It also reflects my understanding of how AI has evolved into today’s data-driven, cloud-powered landscape.

## 💬 Reflection
> Creating this artifact helped me understand how the evolution of AI has been shaped by both breakthroughs and setbacks — from the AI winters to the rise of neural networks.  
> It represents my ability to learn conceptually, think critically, and communicate complex technical topics with clarity and design.
